Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the CrowPanel N95 Backup application and this informational website. The application is a private, single-user utility that runs on its owner's home server. It is not offered to third-party users and does not provide accounts or sign-up.

2. Information accessed

The application uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission allows it to see and manage only files that it creates or that the owner explicitly opens with it. It is used to upload, list and manage the owner's encrypted database backup archives in the owner's own Google Drive.

The application does not read, modify or delete unrelated Google Drive files, and it does not access any other user's Google account.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used solely to transfer and manage the backup archives described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties. It is not used to develop, train or improve machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

CrowPanel N95 Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Google Drive and rclone

Backups are transferred with rclone, an open-source command-line tool running on the owner's server. rclone sends the archives directly to Google Drive through Google's API. The application does not route backup data through any separate storage or processing service operated by its owner.

Google processes data stored in Google Drive under Google's own privacy policy and terms of service.

5. Encryption

Each backup archive is encrypted on the owner's server before it is uploaded. Decryption credentials are managed separately by the owner and are not published on this website or included in the repository that serves it.

6. Credentials and access

OAuth credentials and authorisation tokens remain on the owner's server, protected by the owner's access controls. They are not embedded in this website and are not available to visitors. Access to the application and to backup data is limited to the owner.

7. Third-party users

The application is not offered to third-party users. It does not intentionally collect, process or store personal information belonging to anyone other than its owner, and it does not provide a way for other people to create an account or submit data.

8. Data retention

Encrypted backup archives remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The application does not maintain a separate copy of Google user data on any other service. OAuth authorisation remains active until the owner revokes it through their Google Account permissions or removes the stored token from the server.

This website has no user accounts, cookies or stored visitor data.

9. Website hosting and logging

This website is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect and process standard technical request data in connection with hosting, under GitHub's own privacy statement. This website itself adds no analytics, cookies or tracking scripts.

10. Security

The owner applies access controls to the server, the Google account and the encryption credentials, and backups are encrypted before transfer. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's behaviour changes. The current version is always published at this address.

12. Contact

Because this is a single-user application, data requests are handled directly by the owner. Public questions about this policy can be raised through the issue tracker of the public repository that hosts this site.
